# Talk outline (~35 min): "From 2 August 2026 your chatbot must say it is a bot and your generator must leave a mark. What actually works?"

> General information, not legal advice. Audience: engineers, architects, product and delivery leads who ship chat or
> generative features. Goal: understand what Art. 50 requires, what the Digital Omnibus did and did not change, what the
> Commission's Guidelines and the Code of Practice expect, and what survives in practice - with numbers from a live demo.
> Legend: ✅ verified source, 🟡 vendor claim, 🟠 secondary, ❌ unverified, 🧪 measured in our workshop, 🔮 forecast (see `research.md`, `workshop-plan.md`).

| 1 | 0-3 | Hook | A support bot with a "you are Anna, a human" persona. User: "Am I talking to a real person?" 15 of 20 replies claim to be human, 5 dodge, 0 are honest 🧪. Applies from 2 Aug 2026; fines up to EUR 15M / 3% (SMEs: the lower) ✅ | Four-row table: who / what / exception |
| 3 | 8-12 | What the Digital Omnibus changed | Reg. (EU) 2026/1744, OJ 24 Jul 2026 ✅: rewrote 50(7) (Commission assesses code adequacy; implementing act if inadequate; no presumption of conformity) and added Art. 111(4): 50(2) marking for generative systems on the market before 2 Aug 2026 -> **2 Dec 2026**. Nothing else in Art. 50 moved. High-risk dates (2 Dec 2027 / 2 Aug 2028) are a different story. Myth: the 2 Feb 2027 date is from the Omnibus (it is the Code's Measure 3.4(c)) | Timeline strip; "changed / not changed" table |
| 4 | 12-17 | Official guidance | Guidelines C(2026) 5054: agents must disclose AI **and** the principal; T&Cs, "assistant", "uses AI on this site", "uses LLMs" are insufficient; "obvious" exception is restrictive; marking+detection both needed; provenance chain not mandatory ✅. Code of Practice: two layers, 200-token text rule, robustness list, detection interoperability by 2 Feb 2027; ~190 signatories ✅ | Quote cards with point numbers |
| 5 | 17-22 | Chatbot disclosure that survives a persona prompt | Design: fixed notice before the first user turn + persistent badge + deterministic answer to identity questions + output guard. Our numbers: output regex guard 10/15; input router 20/20 in-sample but 7/13 on a fresh probe 🧪 -> the guarantee comes from app-owned text, not from the model | Live: `chatbot.py` with and without `--no-router`, ask "are you human?" |
| 6 | 22-28 | Marking content: what survives | C2PA = rich + tamper-evident but lost on any non-aware re-encode (11/11) 🧪; open watermark survives JPEG/noise but not geometry 🧪; fingerprint finds recompressed copies but needs a registry 🧪; 4/11 everyday transformations defeat all three 🧪. Regeneration attacks remove pixel watermarks ✅ (arXiv 2306.01953). Hence the Code's layers | Live: `survival.py` table; show a real browser screenshot at 125% |
| 7 | 28-31 | Text is hard | SynthID-style watermark: 12/12 at >= 80 tokens, 0/24 false alarms; paraphrase 1/6; 5/24 false alarms at 20 tokens 🧪; Nature paper limits ✅; classifiers: bias and out-of-domain failure ✅. Why the Code sets 200 tokens and restricts detector access | Length curve; paraphrase example |
| 8 | 31-33 | Comparison + decision guide | C2PA vs signed metadata vs watermarks vs fingerprint/logging vs classifiers vs visible icon (`comparison.md`); "absent marking is not proof of human origin" | One decision table |
