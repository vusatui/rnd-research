# [support] Support channels, SLA and status

Channels: email support on all plans; chat support on Business and Enterprise (priority queue); phone support on Enterprise only.

Target first-response times: Starter 2 business days; Team 1 business day; Business 4 business hours; Enterprise 1 hour for severity-1 incidents, 24/7. Business hours are Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 CET, excluding public holidays in Germany.

Uptime SLA: only Enterprise contracts include a financially backed SLA: 99.95% monthly uptime, with service credits of 10% of the monthly fee below 99.95%, 25% below 99.5% and 50% below 99.0%. Credits must be claimed within 30 days of the incident. Other plans have no SLA.

Status page: status.ledgerly.example shows current incidents and maintenance. Planned maintenance is announced at least 5 days in advance and scheduled on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 CET.

During an incident, support must point customers to the status page and must not speculate about the cause or give an ETA that is not on the status page.

Feature requests are collected at ideas.ledgerly.example; support must not promise delivery dates for unreleased features.

Escalation tags: billing-escalation (charges, refunds disputes), trust-escalation (ownership, identity, legal), bug (reproducible defects; include steps, browser and workspace ID), sales-lead (Enterprise interest, seats > 50, procurement questions).

Language support: English, German, French and Spanish.
