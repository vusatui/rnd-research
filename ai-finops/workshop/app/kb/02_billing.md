# [billing] Billing, invoices from Ledgerly, refunds

Payment methods: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, SEPA Direct Debit (EUR accounts only) and bank transfer for annual plans over $2,000. PayPal is not accepted.

Billing date: monthly plans renew on the same calendar day the subscription started. If that day does not exist in a month (for example the 31st), renewal happens on the last day of that month.

Failed payments: Ledgerly retries a failed card payment 3 times over 7 days (day 1, day 3, day 7) and emails the billing contact each time. If all retries fail, the workspace becomes read-only. Data is never deleted because of a failed payment within the first 60 days; after 60 days of non-payment the workspace is scheduled for deletion with a 14-day final notice.

Refund policy:
- Monthly plans: refundable within 14 days of the charge if fewer than 10 invoices were sent in that billing period.
- Annual plans: full refund within 30 days of the charge; after 30 days, no refund, but the plan can be set to not renew.
- Additional seats are not refundable once the billing period has started.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.
- Only the workspace owner can request a refund, from Settings > Billing > Request refund, or by writing to support from the owner's email address.

Duplicate charges: if a customer reports being charged twice for the same period, support must ask for the last 4 digits of the card and the two charge dates, then escalate to the billing team (tag: billing-escalation). Duplicate charges are always refunded in full.

Ledgerly's own invoices (for the subscription) are available under Settings > Billing > Invoices as PDF. The company name, address and VAT number shown on them can be changed at any time; changes apply to future invoices only. Past invoices cannot be re-issued with a different company name, except to correct a VAT number within 30 days of issue.

Currency of the subscription: USD by default; EUR and GBP are available for workspaces with a billing address in the eurozone or the UK. The subscription currency cannot be changed after the first payment.
