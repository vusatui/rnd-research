# [plans] Plans and pricing

Ledgerly is an invoicing and accounts-receivable SaaS for small and mid-sized businesses. There are four plans.

- **Starter**: $12 per month (or $120 per year). 1 user seat, up to 50 invoices per month, 1 company profile, email support only. No API access.
- **Team**: $39 per month (or $390 per year). Up to 5 user seats included, unlimited invoices, 3 company profiles, recurring invoices, payment reminders, API access with the standard rate limit.
- **Business**: $99 per month (or $990 per year). Up to 15 user seats included, unlimited company profiles, approval workflows, multi-currency (42 currencies), custom invoice templates, priority chat support, API access with the elevated rate limit.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing, minimum 50 seats, annual contract only. Adds SAML single sign-on, SCIM provisioning, audit log export, a 99.95% uptime SLA, a named customer success manager and data residency in the EU or US.

Additional seats: $8 per seat per month on Team, $7 per seat per month on Business. Seats are billed pro rata from the day they are added.

Annual plans are priced at 10 months for 12 (two months free). Prices exclude VAT and sales tax; tax is added at checkout based on the billing address.

Non-profit organisations registered in the EU, UK, US or Canada get 30% off Team and Business after verification (upload the registration certificate under Settings > Billing > Discounts). Discounts cannot be combined.

Plan changes: upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is charged pro rata. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Downgrading to Starter archives company profiles beyond the first one; archived profiles stay readable for 90 days.

Free trial: every new workspace gets a 14-day trial of the Business plan, no credit card required. At the end of the trial the workspace moves to a read-only state until a plan is chosen.

Students and educators: there is no education plan. Point such requests to the Starter plan.
