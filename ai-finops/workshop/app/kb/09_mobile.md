# [mobile] Mobile apps and offline

Ledgerly has iOS (iOS 16 or later) and Android (Android 10 or later) apps. The apps can create, send and view invoices, record payments and scan receipts. Custom templates, approval workflows and integrations settings are web-only.

Offline mode: invoices can be drafted offline; they are synced and can be sent once the device is back online. Sending, payments and exports require a connection.

Receipt scanning extracts amount, date and vendor using on-device OCR; images are uploaded to the workspace only when the expense is saved.

Push notifications: invoice viewed, invoice paid, invoice overdue. They can be switched off per event in the app settings.

Known issue (October 2026): on Android 15 with battery optimisation enabled, push notifications may be delayed by up to 30 minutes. Workaround: exclude Ledgerly from battery optimisation in Android settings.

The apps are free and do not need a separate subscription; they use the workspace's plan.
