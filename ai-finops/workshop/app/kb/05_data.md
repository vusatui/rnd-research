# [data] Data export, import, retention and privacy

Export: owners and admins can export all invoices, customers and payments as CSV or as a full JSON archive from Settings > Data > Export. Exports are generated asynchronously; large archives can take up to 2 hours, and a download link valid for 7 days is emailed when ready. Invoice PDFs can be exported as a ZIP of up to 5,000 files per export.

Import: customers and items can be imported from CSV (max 20,000 rows per file). Historical invoices can be imported from QuickBooks Online, Xero and FreshBooks with the migration assistant; imported invoices keep their original numbers.

Data retention: invoices and payment records are kept for 10 years after the workspace is closed, because tax law in several countries requires it, unless the customer requests deletion and confirms they keep their own copy. Logs are kept for 90 days.

Account deletion (GDPR right to erasure): the owner can delete the workspace from Settings > Data > Delete workspace. Deletion is final after a 30-day grace period during which the workspace can be restored. Individual end-customers of our customers (people who received invoices) who ask Ledgerly to delete their data must be redirected to the business that invoiced them, because that business is the data controller and Ledgerly is the processor.

Data processing agreement (DPA): available to all paid plans, self-serve at Settings > Legal > DPA. It incorporates the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data location: by default data is stored in AWS eu-central-1 (Frankfurt) for workspaces created in the EU/EEA/UK and us-east-2 (Ohio) otherwise. Choosing or moving the region is Enterprise only.

Sub-processors: the current list is published at ledgerly.example/subprocessors; customers subscribed to the list get 30 days' notice before a new sub-processor is added.

Security certifications: SOC 2 Type II (report available under NDA on request) and ISO/IEC 27001. Ledgerly is not HIPAA compliant and does not sign BAAs.

Data access by support: support staff can only access a workspace after the owner grants temporary access (Settings > Security > Support access), which expires automatically after 72 hours.
