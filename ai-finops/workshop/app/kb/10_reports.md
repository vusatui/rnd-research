# [reports] Reports and dashboards

Built-in reports: revenue by month, accounts receivable aging (current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, over 90 days), revenue by customer, tax summary (VAT/GST/sales tax by rate), and payments received. All reports can be filtered by company profile, currency and date range and exported to CSV or PDF.

Aging report basis: by due date by default; can be switched to invoice date in the report settings.

Tax summary: on accrual basis (invoice date) by default; cash basis (payment date) is available on Business and above.

Dashboard: shows outstanding amount, overdue amount, average days to pay (DSO over the last 90 days) and the top 5 overdue customers.

Scheduled reports (Business and above): any report can be emailed weekly or monthly to up to 10 recipients, who do not need a Ledgerly account.

Report data refresh: near real time for invoices and payments; data synced from accounting integrations appears after the next sync (every 15 minutes).
